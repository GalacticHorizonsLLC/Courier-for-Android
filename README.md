Courier is an innovative app that lets you use your S Pen stylus (for Samsung phones) or your finger (for other phones) to take notes directly on your Android lockscreen!

Courier intelligently activates when your S Pen is removed (or when you tap the quick settings tile), allowing you to then draw notes directly on your lockscreen wallpaper, which is then saved as your new wallpaper.

Your last 10 sets of annotated wallpapers are saved with "Courier Recall," which easily lets you restore or view any of those annotations.
